## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**Q: How do I add my first memory?**
1. Open Remembrance
2. Tap the "+" button
3. Select a photo from your library
4. Add a caption or date
5. Tap "Save"

**Q: Can I add multiple photos?**  
Yes! You can add unlimited photos to your Timeline and Gallery.

---

### Subscriptions & Billing

**Q: What's included in Premium?**
- Daily memory reminders with notifications
- Unlimited photo storage
- Inspirational quotes with photos
- Ad-free experience
- Cloud backup

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
1. Open iPhone **Settings**
2. Tap your name at the top
3. Tap **Subscriptions**
4. Select **Remembrance**
5. Tap **Cancel Subscription**

**Q: How do I restore my purchase?**
1. Open Remembrance
2. Go to **Settings** tab
3. Tap **Restore Purchases**
4. Sign in with the same Apple ID

**Q: Will I lose my photos if I cancel?**  
No! Your photos are saved locally. You'll keep all your content but lose Premium features.

---

### Technical Issues

**Q: The app won't open or crashes**
1. Force close the app (swipe up from app switcher)
2. Restart your iPhone
3. Update to the latest iOS version
4. Reinstall Remembrance

**Q: Photos aren't loading**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Go to Settings → Privacy → Photos → Enable for Remembrance
3. Restart the app

**Q: Not receiving daily reminders?**
1. Go to iPhone Settings → Notifications → Remembrance
2. Enable "Allow Notifications"
3. In Remembrance Settings → Enable "Daily Reminders"
