Privacy Policy for Remembrance

Last updated: September 28, 2025

1. Introduction

Remembrance ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our memorial photo application.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Photos: The photos you add to the app are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload, transmit, or have access to your photos.

Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app experience. This data does not personally identify you.

Subscription Information: When you purchase a subscription, Apple handles all payment processing. We only receive confirmation of your subscription status.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain the app functionality
  • To verify your subscription status
  • To send daily memory notifications (if enabled)
  • To improve and optimize the app experience

4. Data Storage and Security

All photos and personal data remain on your device. We do not have servers that store your personal information. Your photos are as secure as your device itself.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Apple's StoreKit for subscription management. Apple's privacy policy applies to payment processing and subscription management.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • You can delete photos at any time from the gallery
  • You can disable notifications in settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Email: support@remembranceapp.com

10. Consent

By using Remembrance, you consent to our Privacy Policy.
