# Finding Agreements in App Store Connect

## Method 1: From User Menu
1. Click on your name/profile icon in the top-right corner
2. Select "Agreements, Tax, and Banking" from the dropdown

## Method 2: From Business Section
1. Look at the very top navigation bar (not the left sidebar)
2. You should see tabs like: "My Apps", "App Analytics", "Sales and Trends", etc.
3. Click on "Business" or "Agreements, Tax, and Banking"

## Method 3: Direct URL
Go directly to: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/agreements

## What You're Looking For:
Once you're in Agreements:
1. Look for "Paid Applications" section
2. It should show either:
   - "Active" (good - already accepted)
   - "Action Needed" or "Agreement Update" (needs acceptance)
   - Missing entirely (needs to be set up)

## Important Notes:
- Only the **Account Holder** (the person who created the developer account) can accept agreements
- If you're not the Account Holder, you'll need them to sign in and accept
- The account needs to be enrolled in the Apple Developer Program (not just App Store Connect user)

## If Still Can't Find:
1. Check your role: Users and Access → find your email → check "Role"
   - Must be "Account Holder" or "Admin" to see some options
2. Verify you're in the right team if you belong to multiple
3. The developer account must be fully activated with Apple Developer Program membership

## Alternative Check:
You can also verify by trying to create an in-app purchase:
1. Go to your app → In-App Purchases
2. Try to create a new one
3. If agreements aren't accepted, it will show an error message
