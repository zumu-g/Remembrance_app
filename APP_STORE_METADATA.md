## App Description

Keep cherished memories alive with Remembrance, a beautiful memorial photo app designed to celebrate the life of someone special. Each day, view a carefully selected photo and inspirational quote that helps you remember and honor your loved one.

**Key Features:**
• Daily Photo Selection - A new memory each day from your collection
• Inspirational Quotes - 365 unique quotes about love, hope, and remembrance
• Timeline View - Browse through past daily memories
• Photo Gallery - Store up to 500 memorial photos
• Daily Reminders - Optional notifications for your daily memory
• Beautiful Design - Elegant, respectful interface with customizable themes

**Perfect for:**
• Creating a digital memorial space
• Daily remembrance rituals
• Grief support and healing
• Celebrating a life well-lived
• Keeping memories close
